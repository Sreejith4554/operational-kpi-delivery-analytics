# Executive Story

A useful management sequence is:

1. **Start with exceptions:** Red projects, high/critical RAID items and positive schedule variance.
2. **Separate symptoms from causes:** schedule slippage may be driven by dependency delay, capacity pressure or unresolved decisions.
3. **Name the owner and decision:** every exception should have a person accountable for the next action.
4. **Track recovery:** once corrective action is agreed, monitor whether forecast variance is stabilising or worsening.

## Interview framing

“I designed the model around questions leaders actually ask. The first screen surfaces exceptions, then the underlying data lets me explain why a project is Amber or Red. I would validate the source data before publishing because a visually strong dashboard is still poor governance if the dates, ownership or status logic are inconsistent.”
