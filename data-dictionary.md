# Data Dictionary

## Portfolio
- `baseline_finish` — approved target finish date used for variance comparison.
- `forecast_finish` — current expected finish date.
- `rag_status` — management health indicator (Green/Amber/Red).
- `completion_pct` — management estimate of overall progress.
- `planned_cost` / `actual_cost` — synthetic cost values for budget variance.
- `completed_deliverables` / `planned_deliverables` — simple delivery throughput measure.

## RAID
- `type` — Risk, Issue, Action or Dependency.
- `severity` — Low, Medium, High or Critical.
- `status` — Open or Closed.
- `due_date` — date by which mitigation, resolution or delivery is expected.

## Resources
- `capacity_fte` — available full-time-equivalent capacity.
- `demand_fte` — requested workload equivalent.
